Platinum Roadmap — 6 steps, one playthrough

Estimated total: roughly 150–200 hours in a single playthrough. No online play is required, and nothing besides Positive Mental Attitude can be missed — so relax and play the story first.

  1. Play the story, enjoy the ride

    Chapters 1–5 take around 50–60 hours and hand you every chapter and named-mission trophy, plus the vague story trophies, on their own. Explore freely as you go — and meditate at every meditation spot the moment you find it, because Positive Mental Attitude is the game's one missable trophy.

  2. Close out the side strands

    Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, take Bret's unique-animal hunt, and complete 8 full Strangers & Freaks mission strands for the Silver. These characters also unlock useful services and income for the grind ahead.

  3. Sweep the activities

    250 of the 397 stunts, gold medals in all 42 shooting range challenges, a Top Fun drone race win at all 14 locations, 20 different race wins, an arcade game, Cal's fishing challenge, 50 challenges, and a taste of all 9 activity types for Master of None.

  4. Go on a crime spree

    Rob 5 bank vaults and escape, hit an armored truck, rob a jewelry store without being detected, chain 3 shop robberies back-to-back, rob an illegal operation belonging to each gang, take down 5 enemies in one Combat Focus, and lose a 4-star wanted level with your partner.

  5. Collect, register, spend

    Complete 3 collectible card sets, scan 100 locations with WAiNK, discover 100 vehicles, buy all 8 garages ($160,000 total), register 10 vehicles to Scooter Bros, spend $100,000 across shops, launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, and explore every corner of Leonida.

  6. The 100% push

    Follow the in-game checklist (Touch Pad > Progress > 100% Completion) until A Long Road pops. Vice Got a Price! — the Platinum — follows the moment your last trophy lands. Welcome to the legend club.
